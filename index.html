OUR TAKE

For value and comfort through Asia: Qantas plus SWISS, with Premium Economy only on Singapore–Zurich, is about A$5,403 per person. For the lowest economy fare: Emirates is A$3,275 each, with the Dubai safety considerations below. Singapore Airlines offers the simplest Asia booking. Hong Kong is the expensive choice at these exact dates.

Fares are a snapshot, with no seats held. Hotels, insurance, transfers and trains are extra. Currency conversions and card fees can change the final Australian-dollar amount. Premium upgrades below refer to the named individual flight, unless explicitly described as a bundle. A more expensive fare can also change baggage, seat selection and cancellation terms. Each single-leg comparison includes the fare conditions offered for that cabin. Supplements are differences between fares when booking now. Post-purchase upgrade offers were not priced.

WHAT THE UPGRADE ACTUALLY MEANS

Choose comfort one flight at a time

Premium economy is a separate cabin with a wider reclining seat and more legroom. It does not provide a lie-flat bed. Lounge access usually costs extra unless another entitlement applies.

Want premium between Brisbane and Singapore?

The selected Qantas flights and SQ236/SQ245 have no Premium Economy cabin. Singapore Airlines has two alternative nonstop services with that cabin:

  • SQ266: Brisbane 29 November 18:10 → Singapore 30 November 00:05, Airbus A350-900 Long Haul.
  • SQ265: Singapore 6 January 07:05 → Brisbane 16:55, Airbus A350-900 Long Haul.

A direct Singapore Airlines return using those flights was A$6,941.96 for two in economy. Making only SQ266 premium increased it to A$8,043.96 for two, an extra A$551 per person. SQ265 stays in economy. SQ265 has a Premium Economy cabin fitted, but no premium fare for two adults was available in the checked return or standalone searches.

This can replace the Qantas booking in option 2. With only Brisbane–Singapore in premium and both SWISS flights in economy, the complete trip is approximately A$6,390 per person. That is about A$1,406 more each than the Qantas-based economy option, because both the airline fare and the outbound cabin change. You can separately add SWISS premium on either Europe flight at its listed supplement.

This alternative arrives in Singapore after midnight on 30 November and leaves for Europe just after midnight on 1 December. For immediate room access after arrival, book the hotel from 29 November. THE MIDDLE EAST STOPOVER

Dubai is feasible, with a meaningful safety trade-off

Australian advice: exercise a high degree of caution

Checked on 10 November 2026, Smartraveller warns of regional conflict, possible attacks and sudden airspace closures or flight cancellations. Conditions can change before departure. The same advice level currently applies to Qatar.

A city stopover is practical: Emirates sells Dubai stopover arrangements and Australian passport holders are currently eligible for a tourist visa on arrival. Hotel and transfer costs are additional. The planned two-night stays are not automatically covered by complimentary Dubai Connect.

Cost: the airfare is lower in this search. No dated Dubai hotel quote is included, so the complete saving after four hotel nights and transfers remains unconfirmed. Early morning arrival on 4 January may require paid early check-in or booking the previous night.

Our judgement: choose Dubai only if both parents are comfortable with the current regional risk and the insurer confirms relevant cover and exclusions. For a more settled holiday plan, the Singapore routes remain attractive despite the higher fare.

BEFORE CHOOSING

A few details worth getting right

Midnight departures

The Singapore–Europe flights depart just after midnight on 1 December. Go to the airport on the evening of Monday 30 November. A hotel booked for 29 November to 1 December gives room access on the 30th, with departure that evening. It provides one full night’s sleep before the overnight flight.

Separate airline tickets

Qantas plus SWISS involves two bookings. The Hong Kong combination uses the bookings listed in its section. A change to one ticket does not automatically change the others. The long stopovers provide a buffer, though disruption can still affect later flights.

Hotels and onward travel

Stopover hotels, transfers, insurance and Swiss rail travel are extra. Collect bags and check in again after each city stay. For mixed cabins and separate tickets, pack to the lowest checked-baggage allowance shown on the tickets.
